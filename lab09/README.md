### Statement conventions

- **C:** The wording says “right”, but its definition (`qwerty` shifted by 2 becomes `ertyqw`) and sample use left shifts. The solution follows that convention and finds the first occurrence of the original string in the doubled changed string.
- **D:** City names are compared without regard to capitalization, as required by the examples; their original capitalization is preserved in the output.
- **F:** The example gives the text first and the pattern second, with 1-based occurrence positions. The solution follows this order.
- **H:** The equation reduces to `a = b`. The solution uses the linear-time Z algorithm to count equal adjacent prefixes while leaving a nonempty third part.
